Good to know

Before you set off.

Clear answers for a smoother start.

How do I find a bus service?

Enter your departure city, destination and travel date, then choose Discover services. Popular journey cards can fill in the cities for you.

Why are no services appearing?

Only active services configured in the connected database are shown. If the operator inventory has not been added, your search may return no results.

Can I book seats and pay online right now?

Not yet. This build can search configured services, but it does not currently lock seats, process payments or issue confirmed tickets.

Are fares and seat counts live?

They reflect records currently configured by the operator. They should not be treated as live inventory until a verified operator integration is connected.

Booking transparency: HEPRA BUS is currently an early-access booking experience. Live operator inventory, transactional seat holds, secure payment processing and ticket issuance are not yet enabled. No payment is taken through this page.
