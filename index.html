Frequently asked questions

What to know before you search.

How do I search for a bus?

Enter your starting city, destination and travel date, then select Search buses. Popular route cards can fill in the cities for you.

Why are no buses appearing?

Only services configured in the connected database are displayed. If operator inventory has not been added, the search can return no results.

Can I reserve a seat or pay online?

Not yet. Seat locking, payment processing and ticket confirmation are not enabled in this early-access build.

Are fares and availability live?

They reflect the records currently configured by the service operator. They should not be treated as live inventory until a verified operator integration is connected.

Early-access build: this website is a booking-flow starter. Real operator inventory, seat locking, ticket confirmation and online payments are not enabled. No payment is taken through this page.
